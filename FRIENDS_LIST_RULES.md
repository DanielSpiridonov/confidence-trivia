# Friends List Rules

This social/account feature is separate from the future Friends Mode game mode.

- Friends require registered accounts and unique permanent player names.
- Friend requests may be accepted, rejected, removed, or blocked.
- An account may have at most 25 accepted friends.
- A player may send one free gift to each accepted friend per UTC calendar day.
- Gifts cost the sender nothing and award exactly 2 stars to the receiver.
- Each direction is independent: two friends may gift each other on the same day.
- A receiver may receive one gift from every accepted friend each day.
- Gifts must be explicitly claimed and can only be claimed once.
- Unclaimed gifts expire when the UTC day changes.
- Pending, removed, or blocked friendships cannot send or claim gifts.
- Friendship, gift, and star mutations are server-authoritative and inaccessible through direct public database policies.
