# Confivia Support

For gameplay, account, safety, or technical assistance, contact
spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. To help us investigate, include only what is
necessary:

- your player name or account identifier;
- device platform, device model, and operating-system version;
- game mode and approximate date and time of the problem; and
- a short description and, if useful, a redacted screenshot.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, sign-in tokens, payment information, or
another person's private information. Remove unrelated personal information
from screenshots.

## Common help

- **Connection problems:** check your internet connection, restart Confivia,
  and try joining the room again.
- **Sign-in problems:** confirm that you are using the same Apple or Google
  account previously connected to the Confivia profile.
- **Real-money purchases:** version one does not support them. Do not send
  payment information to Confivia support.

## Safety reports

Use the in-app reporting option where one is available. If it is unavailable,
email spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com with the relevant player name, approximate
date and time, and a brief description.
