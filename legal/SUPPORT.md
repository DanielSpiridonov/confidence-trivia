# Confidence Trivia Support

For gameplay, account, safety, or technical assistance, contact
spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. Include:

- your player name;
- device platform and operating-system version;
- the game mode and approximate time of the problem;
- a short description and screenshot, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens, or other people's private information.

## Safety reports

Use **Settings > Report player** for offensive names or player conduct. You may
also contact spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com if the in-app form is unavailable.
