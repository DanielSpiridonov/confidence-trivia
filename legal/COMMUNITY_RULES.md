# Confivia Community Rules

Confivia should remain welcoming and fair. These rules apply to player names,
Custom-room questions and answers, friend interactions, challenges,
multiplayer games, safety reports, and the official Confivia Discord community.

## Be respectful

Do not use hateful, sexually explicit, threatening, harassing, discriminatory,
or deliberately offensive names or conduct. Do not impersonate another person,
organisation, moderator, or official Confivia account. Do not share another
person's private information.

## Play fairly

Do not use automation, exploits, modified clients, coordinated match fixing, or
other methods to manipulate games, rankings, stars, rewards, wagers, or queues.
Do not interfere with the service or another player's ability to participate.

## Report responsibly

Use the in-app reporting option where one is available. If you cannot access
it, send a concise report to spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. Include the relevant
player name and approximate date and time, but do not send passwords, access
tokens, payment information, or unnecessary personal information.

Reports must be truthful. Knowingly false or abusive reports may be treated as
misconduct.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, we may require a name change, correct
improperly obtained progression, restrict features, temporarily suspend an
account, or permanently terminate access. Serious conduct may result in
immediate action.

Questions or reasonable appeals may be sent to
spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to identify the
relevant account and decision.

## Official community

The official community is https://discord.gg/BjcjJGSyxv. Discord is an external
service with its own rules, terms, and privacy policy. Never post account,
deletion, privacy, or other sensitive requests publicly in Discord.
