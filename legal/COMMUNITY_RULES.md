# Confidence Trivia Community Rules

Confidence Trivia should remain welcoming and fair. These rules apply to player
names, friend interactions, challenges, reports, and multiplayer games.

## Be respectful

Do not use hateful, sexually explicit, threatening, harassing, discriminatory,
or deliberately offensive names or conduct. Do not impersonate another person,
organisation, moderator, or official account.

## Play fairly

Do not use automation, exploits, modified clients, coordinated match fixing, or
other methods to manipulate games, rankings, stars, rewards, wagers, or queues.
Do not repeatedly send unwanted friend requests or challenges.

## Report responsibly

Use the in-app report form when a player name or interaction violates these
rules. Reports should be truthful and concise. Repeated knowingly false or
abusive reports may themselves be treated as misconduct.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, we may require a name change, remove social
access, temporarily suspend an account, correct improperly obtained progression,
or permanently ban an account. Serious conduct may result in immediate action.
Blocking prevents further friend and challenge access between the players.

Questions or appeals may be sent to spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com.
