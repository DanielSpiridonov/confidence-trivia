# Privacy Policy for Confivia

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Last updated:** 1 October 2026

Confivia is provided by Daniel Spiridonov, an independent developer established
in Bulgaria ("we", "us", or "our"). We are the controller of the personal data
described in this policy. You can contact us at spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com or
at Oborishte 108, Bulgaria.

This policy explains what information Confivia processes, why it is processed,
how long it is kept, who receives it, and the rights available to you.

## Information we process

- **Account information:** Supabase user identifier, sign-in provider, and the
  email address supplied when you use Apple or Google sign-in. If Apple provides
  a private relay address, we receive that address instead of your personal
  address.
- **Installation identity:** a randomly generated device identifier used during
  onboarding, account linking, and session recovery. Version one requires a
  connected Apple or Google account before gameplay begins.
- **Profile and customisation:** player name and owned or equipped avatars,
  name colours, and frames.
- **Gameplay and progression:** answers, scores, placements, match results,
  progression, stars, reward eligibility, wagers, and transaction-ledger
  records needed to operate the game and prevent duplicate rewards.
- **Custom games:** questions and expected answers entered by a Custom-room
  host are transmitted to the game server and displayed to that room's players.
- **Social features:** friend requests and relationships, blocks, Blessings,
  challenges, and presence or last-seen information used by the Friends screen.
- **Support and safety information:** information you choose to include in a
  support, privacy, deletion, or safety request, such as your player name,
  message, device details, and screenshots.
- **Technical information:** IP address, connection events, request counters,
  and limited server or infrastructure logs needed to deliver and secure the
  service.
- **Device-only preferences:** sound, music, haptic, and accessibility settings
  are stored locally and are not intentionally sent to us.

Version one does not intentionally collect contacts, precise location, photos,
health information, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. It does
not include third-party advertising, advertising analytics, or real-money
purchases.

## Required and optional information

An account identifier, installation identifier, player name, gameplay data, and
basic connection data are required to provide the corresponding online game
features. If you do not provide them, those features cannot operate. A supported
Apple or Google account must be connected before entering the game. Information in support
messages and screenshots is voluntary; include only what is necessary for us to
handle your request.

## Purposes and legal bases

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and other places where
similar rules apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Create and manage accounts; operate multiplayer and Custom games; preserve progression, stars, inventory, social features, and results | Performance of our agreement with you |
| Authenticate users and respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests | Performance of our agreement with you and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations |
| Secure the service; prevent cheating, fraud, duplicate rewards, abuse, and technical failures | Our legitimate interests in protecting Confivia, its players, and its virtual economy |
| Maintain limited records needed for disputes, enforcement, accounting, or other legal requirements | Compliance with legal obligations and our legitimate interests in establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims |

We do not rely on consent for advertising or cross-app tracking in version one
because those activities are not used. Where we introduce an optional feature
that legally requires consent, we will request it separately and allow it to be
withdrawn.

## Information visible to other players

Your player name, avatar, frame, name colour, gameplay actions, scores, and
relevant match results may be shown to other participants. Custom questions and
answers are shown to players in that room. Friends may see relationship,
Blessing, challenge, and availability information needed for those features.
Your email address, sign-in provider identity, installation identifier, IP address,
and support messages are not displayed to other players.

## Service providers

We use the following processors to provide Confivia:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and related backend services. The
  production database is configured in the Central EU (Frankfurt) region.
- **Render** — game-server hosting. The production game server is configured in
  Oregon, United States.
- **Apple and Google** — sign-in services when you choose the corresponding
  provider. They also process information independently under their own terms
  and privacy policies.

If you follow our Discord invitation, Discord processes your use of its service
under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not post account, deletion, or privacy
requests publicly in Discord.

We do not sell personal information. We do not permit our processors to use
Confivia player data for their own advertising.

## International transfers

Some information is processed outside Bulgaria or the European Economic Area,
including on Render infrastructure in the United States. Where required, these
transfers are protected through an applicable adequacy mechanism, the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and supplementary contractual or
technical measures. Supabase and Render make data-processing terms available
that include transfer safeguards. You may contact us for additional information
about the safeguards relevant to your data.

## Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above:

- registered profile, progression, stars, inventory, and account data are kept
  while the account remains active;
- temporary, unlinked installation records are removed after 30 days without
  activity, unless a record must be kept for match, currency, safety, or other
  integrity reasons;
- friendships, blocks, unclaimed Blessings, challenges, and presence data are
  kept while operationally needed or until the associated account is deleted;
- Custom-room questions and expected answers ordinarily remain only in active
  room memory and are not intentionally retained after the room ends;
- completed match results and Ranked progression audit records are stored to
  maintain progression, fair rankings, and reward integrity. They currently
  have no automatic expiry. On account deletion, player names in match results
  are replaced and limited integrity records may remain as described below;
- support and safety correspondence is ordinarily kept for up to 30 days after
  the request is closed, unless longer retention is needed for a dispute,
  enforcement action, or legal obligation;
- application runtime logs held by Render expire after 7 to 30 days depending
  on the hosting workspace plan; and
- transaction-ledger and promotional-redemption records may be kept while the
  account exists, and for a limited period afterward where necessary to protect
  currency integrity, prevent duplicate rewards, resolve disputes, or comply
  with law.

These periods must reflect the production database, backup, and hosting
configuration. We may retain limited information longer where reasonably
necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution,
or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

When an account is deleted, directly identifying account, profile, and cosmetic
data are removed immediately. A daily cleanup removes the remaining
deleted-account database row and its player-linked match and Ranked result rows
once 30 days have elapsed. Retained currency and wager audit records have their
reference to that player cleared. Limited match or transaction records may remain only where
there is a valid reason above and will be de-identified where identification is
no longer necessary. De-identified data is not described as anonymous unless it
can no longer reasonably be linked back to a person.

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data and receive a copy;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive certain data in a portable format; and
- withdraw consent where a particular activity relies on consent, without
  affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. We may request reasonable
information to verify that the request concerns your account. You also have the
right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection at
https://cpdp.bg/en/ or to another competent supervisory authority in your place
of residence.

## Account deletion

Open **Profile > Delete account** in Confivia to request permanent deletion. If
you cannot access the app, follow the instructions at
https://daniel-portfolio-pied.vercel.app/projects/confivia/delete-account.
Deleting the app from a device does not by
itself delete a registered account. The separate deletion page explains how
registered accounts are handled and what limited records may remain.

## Children

Confivia is intended only for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly
collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe that a person under
16 has provided personal data, contact us so that we can investigate and delete
it where appropriate.

The store age rating describes game content and does not replace this minimum
age requirement.

## Security

We use HTTPS/WSS encryption in transit, authenticated access controls,
restricted database permissions, rate limits, and token-safe logging practices.
No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
