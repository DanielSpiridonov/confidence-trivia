# Privacy Policy for Confidence Trivia

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]  
**Last updated:** [LAST UPDATED DATE]

Confidence Trivia is provided by Daniel Spiridonov, an independent developer
based in Bulgaria ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what information
we process, why we process it, and the choices available to you. Questions and
privacy requests may be sent to spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com.

## Information we process

- **Account and identity information:** player name, Supabase user identifier,
  sign-in provider, and email address when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  If Apple supplies a private relay address, we receive that relay address.
- **Guest identity:** a randomly generated installation identifier used to keep
  guest progress and reconnect game sessions.
- **Gameplay and progression:** answers, scores, placements, match history,
  rank, LP, ranked wins, stars, reward eligibility, wagers, and transaction
  ledger records.
- **Customisation:** owned and equipped avatars, name colours, and frames.
- **Social and safety information:** friendships, Blessings, challenges,
  presence/last-seen information, blocks, player reports, report descriptions,
  and moderation decisions.
- **Technical information:** network address and short-term request counters may
  be processed by our server and hosting providers for operation, security, and
  abuse prevention.
- **Device-only preferences:** language, sound, music, haptic, and accessibility
  preferences are stored locally and are not intentionally sent to us.

Version one does not intentionally collect contacts, precise location, photos,
health information, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. It does
not include advertising analytics or real-money purchases.

## How we use information

We use this information to operate multiplayer games; authenticate accounts;
restore progress across devices; maintain rankings, stars, rewards, wagers, and
inventory; provide friends and challenges; prevent fraud and duplicate rewards;
moderate player names and reported conduct; secure and troubleshoot the service;
and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for
cross-app tracking or targeted advertising in version one.

## When information is visible to others

Your player name, avatar, frame, name colour, gameplay actions, scores, and
relevant results may be shown to other players. Ranked leaderboard entries may
show player name, rank, LP, position, and ranked wins. Your email address and
sign-in identity are not displayed to other players. Blocking and reporting do
not disclose the reporter's identity to the reported player.

## Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication and database services and Render for game
server hosting. Apple and Google process information when their sign-in services
are used. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy
commitments. Production data is hosted through Supabase in Central EU
(Frankfurt) and through Render in Oregon, United States, and may be processed
in other countries as necessary to provide these services
and lawful support operations.

## Retention

Registered profile data, stars, inventory, friendships, rank, and aggregate
progress are retained while the account exists. Promo redemption and star/wager
ledger records may be retained while the account exists where needed to prevent
duplicate rewards and maintain currency integrity.

Our version-one retention schedule is:

- inactive guest records without a continuing integrity or moderation need:
  up to 30 days;
- presence data, completed or expired challenges, and claimed Blessings: up to
  30 days after they are no longer operationally needed;
- resolved or dismissed player-report descriptions: up to 30 days after
  resolution; pending reports remain until reviewed;
- detailed ordinary match records: up to 30 days, after which only necessary
  aggregate progression or anonymised integrity history may remain;
- application logs: no more than 30 days.

We may retain limited information longer where reasonably necessary for fraud
prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement. On
account deletion, authentication, profile, social, and cosmetic data are
removed; completed match and transaction history may remain only in anonymised
form under a generic deleted-user identity.

## Your choices and rights

You may sign out, block players, submit a report, and delete your account from
inside the app. You may also use [PUBLIC SITE URL]/delete-account or contact
spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access,
correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to
object to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before acting
on a request.

## Account deletion

In the app, open Profile and choose **Delete account**. This permanently removes
the account and associated personal data except for limited anonymised or
legally required records described above. See [PUBLIC SITE URL]/delete-account
for the web request route.

## Children

Confidence Trivia is not intended for children under 13. Where local law sets a
higher age at which a child may independently consent to relevant data
processing, a parent or guardian must provide any legally required permission.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children contrary to
these requirements. If you believe a child has done so, contact
spiridonovdaniels@gmail.com.

## Security

We use encrypted HTTPS/WSS transport, authenticated access controls, restricted
database access, request rate limits, and token-safe logging practices. No
method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. We will
change the date above and provide appropriate notice for material changes.
